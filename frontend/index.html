What is Tet?

Tet is a Web3 platform built for a new era of crypto transactions. It gives you a secure, transparent place to make a deal without relying on trust alone: your funds sit in a smart contract, not in someone's account. Use it for freelance work, real-world asset trades, OTC deals, or any agreement between two parties.
