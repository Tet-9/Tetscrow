Products

Built for both sides of the deal

Whether you're paying for work or delivering it, Tet gives you the same guarantee: the contract enforces the deal, not a person's word.

◆

For Sender

Define the scope, set your milestones, and lock funds in escrow. Your payment only leaves the contract when you approve delivery — or when an arbiter rules in your favor during a dispute. You're never sending money on a promise.

◇

For Receiver

Know the funds are already locked on-chain before you start work — verifiable by you, not just claimed by the other side. Deliver, mark the milestone complete, and get paid automatically on approval.

What is Tet?

Escrow, without the escrow agent

Tet replaces the traditional third-party escrow agent with a transparent, auditable smart contract. Every agreement, deposit, and release is a public on-chain event — no black box, no "trust us."

🔒

Tet is Secure

Funds are held by an open-source contract, not a company account. Security-first design from fund locking to fee handling.

🌐

Tet is Decentralized

No central custodian holds your money. Settlement logic runs on Ethereum, visible to anyone who wants to check it.

⚙

Tet is Smart

Milestones, disputes, and arbitration are all encoded — the contract does exactly what it says, every time.

Real World Asset Services

Beyond digital work

Tet's milestone-and-arbitration model isn't limited to freelance or digital deliverables — the same agreement structure (define scope → lock funds → confirm delivery → release or arbitrate) applies to real-world asset transactions, with the terms and delivery evidence referenced via the agreement's termsURI and dispute record.

How Tet Works

Five steps, start to finish

1

Create a Mutual Agreement

Sender defines the receiver, amount, arbiter, and terms. Nothing moves yet.

2

Fund the Escrow

Sender deposits ETH (or an approved token) directly into the contract.

3

Deliver

Receiver completes the work and marks the milestone delivered.

4

Release — or Dispute

Sender approves and funds release automatically, or either party raises a dispute.

5

Arbitration (if needed)

The designated arbiter reviews the case on-chain and resolves it — funds move to the winner.
