For Sender

Gain Trust, Show Commitment. Here is what the sending side does on Tet.

Open App How Tet Works
1

Create the agreement

Enter the receiver's wallet address, the amount, an arbiter if you want a specific one, and a link to the written terms.

2

Fund the escrow

Deposit the agreed amount. It is locked in the smart contract, so neither Tet nor the receiver can touch it until the deal resolves.

3

Wait for delivery

Follow the agreement's status on your dashboard. It changes to Delivered when the receiver marks the work done.

4

Review and release

Check the work against the terms. Release Funds sends the payment to the receiver, minus the platform fee. Releasing is final, so review first.

5

Dispute if needed

If the work does not match the agreement, raise a dispute before you release. The funds freeze and the arbiter decides who receives them.
