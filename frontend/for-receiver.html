For Receiver

Receive Payment Safely, Feel Secure. Here is what the receiving side does on Tet.

Open App How Tet Works
1

Check the escrow is funded

Before you start, open the agreement and confirm its status is Funded. The money is already locked in the contract, and you can verify that on-chain yourself.

2

Do the work

Deliver the service or asset exactly as described in the agreement's terms. If anything is unclear, settle it with the sender before you begin.

3

Mark it delivered

Press Mark Delivered in the app when you are done. This records the delivery on-chain and starts the auto-release timer.

4

Get paid

The sender releases the payment and the funds go to your wallet. If the sender stays silent, anyone can trigger auto-release once the window passes, and the funds go to you.

5

If there is a problem

Either side can raise a dispute, and the arbiter reviews the case and pays the rightful party. Keep your evidence ready: messages, files and proof of delivery.
